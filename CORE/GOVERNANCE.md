# Governance and Intellectual Property

## Purpose
This repository stores original McGreen methods and attributed research. It is not a mirror of third-party repositories or a collection of material without review.

## Source handling
For every upstream source, record project and owner, canonical URL, license, version/release/commit, date checked, relevant material inspected, capabilities and limitations, what was learned, restrictions, and attribution obligations.

## License rules
- Public availability on GitHub does not mean public-domain or unrestricted reuse.
- Inspect the actual license and applicable notices.
- If no license is present, treat the material as copyrighted; do not copy or redistribute it.
- Track code, documentation, datasets, fonts, models, textures, images, and other assets separately.
- Preserve required notices and attribution when reuse is permitted.
- Do not combine materials with incompatible licenses without review.
- A repository-wide license for McGreen-authored work will be selected only after reviewing the contents and intended reuse.

Attribution does not itself grant permission to copy. McGreen skills must be independently written. Research may inform general principles and original conclusions, but distinctive wording, code, diagrams, datasets, workflows, or assets must not be reproduced without a valid basis.

## Evidence labels
- **Verified:** checked against a primary source or reproducible test.
- **Observed:** seen in a tool or workflow but not independently validated.
- **Synthesized:** original conclusion derived from multiple inputs.
- **Hypothesis:** plausible but not yet tested.
- **Deprecated:** retained for history but no longer recommended.

## Change control
Meaningful changes should record why they are needed, what changed, evidence or test results, known limitations, date, and version. Do not silently replace a tested method with an untested one.

## Responsibility
Respect privacy, confidentiality, consent, accessibility, physical safety, applicable regulations, and platform terms. AI-generated output is not a substitute for qualified legal, engineering, construction, or safety review.

# Research Method

## Objective
Study relevant tools, repositories, documentation, and professional references; understand their capabilities and constraints; then convert verified learning into original McGreen knowledge and methods.

## Sequence
1. Define the specific capability or design question.
2. Find primary sources: official repositories, manuals, specifications, release notes, and original research.
3. Check provenance: owner, maintenance signals, license, release/commit, and date.
4. Inspect relevant documentation and files. Do not infer capabilities from a project name or README alone.
5. Test where feasible. Distinguish documented claims from capabilities actually tested.
6. Compare alternatives on strengths, weaknesses, prerequisites, cost, hardware, and workflow fit.
7. Synthesize independently in McGreen's own structure and language.
8. Create or revise a skill with inputs, procedure, decision points, outputs, and checks.
9. Evaluate on a representative task and record evidence.
10. Revisit sources when relevant releases or changes occur.

## Source priority
1. Official upstream repository and documentation
2. Standards bodies and primary technical specifications
3. Original academic or professional research
4. Maintainer-authored technical material
5. Reputable independent analysis
6. Community discussion as leads, not final authority

## Required research record
Use UPSTREAM-REGISTRY.md. A source is not reviewed merely because its repository page was opened.

## Synthesis rules
Extract concepts, not distinctive prose. Compare approaches where useful. Record what does not transfer to McGreen's use. Label uncertainty. Link each resulting skill to the research records that informed it.

## Practical evaluation record
- Environment and version
- Inputs and settings
- Steps performed
- Expected and actual result
- Defects and constraints
- Time, compute, and cost where relevant
- Reproducibility notes

A workflow is **validated** only after its stated acceptance criteria are met and evidence is recorded.
